Privacy Policy

Prisme processes your answers, your portrait and your history directly on your device.

Locally stored data: your preferences and your history stay on your phone (local storage). This data is not sent to our servers. We ask for no account and no directly identifying personal data.

Anonymous audience measurement with Pulse: each time the app opens, it sends our Pulse service the app name, version, platform and language solely to measure audience. Pulse sets no cookie and the app sends no user or device identifier. A daily unique visitor is calculated server-side from the request’s technical data using a salted, non-reversible hash; only that hash is stored for this count.

Advertising: the app is free and funded by ads served through Google AdMob, including an optional rewarded video you can always decline — your portrait is shown either way. AdMob may use advertising identifiers to serve ads; you can disable personalisation in your device settings. There is no in-app purchase and no subscription.

Duo: comparing two portraits happens entirely on your two devices. The portrait summary travels inside the link or the code you share yourself, in the part after the “#”, which browsers never send to a server. Nothing is uploaded, nothing about your duo is stored on our side and we never see it. No account is needed and we build no profile about you.

Contact: for any question, email us at support@dcyou.co.
