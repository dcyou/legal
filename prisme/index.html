Privacy Policy

Prisme processes your answers, your portrait and your history directly on your device.

Locally stored data: your preferences and your history stay on your phone (local storage). This data is not sent to our servers. We ask for no account and no directly identifying personal data.

Audience measurement (Pulse, our own statistics server, hosted by Laravel Cloud): each time the app starts, it sends a single event containing the app name, its version, the platform (iOS, Android or web), your device’s language setting and the event name (“open”) — no identifier, no cookie. The server stores these fields with the date and time, plus a daily fingerprint: a SHA-256 hash of your IP address, the user agent of the app’s web view, the app name, the date and a secret key. This fingerprint counts unique daily visitors, changes every day and differs from one app to another; your IP address and user agent are not written into the events. To limit abusive requests, the server also keeps a request counter in its cache, tied to a SHA-1 hash of your IP address (without a secret key); this entry is meant to expire after one minute, but its automatic erasure is not guaranteed. Our host, Laravel Cloud, may also record IP addresses in its technical access logs, under its own retention rules. We only look at totals (launches and unique visitors per app). These events are not automatically deleted at this time.

Advertising: the app is free and funded by ads served through Google AdMob. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent through Google’s own form (UMP) before any ad is requested; if this step cannot be completed (for example without a network connection), no ad is requested and the step is tried again later; if you refuse consent, the app still shows ads, but Google then serves only limited, non-personalised ads. The ads are non-personalised: the app never asks for permission to track you, and shows no tracking prompt on any platform. Where Google requires it, Settings carries an “Ad privacy settings” entry that reopens that form so you can change or withdraw your choice at any time. The Google Mobile Ads SDK collects your IP address (which can be used to estimate your approximate location), device identifiers (on Android, the advertising ID and the app set ID; on iOS, only an identifier tied to the app’s publisher, never the advertising identifier), your interactions with the app and the ads (app launches, taps, ads viewed) and diagnostic and performance data (such as launch time and crashes). Google uses this data, encrypted in transit, to serve and measure ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Rewarded video: an optional short video is offered before your portrait. It never starts on its own, and declining it changes nothing — the six chapters of your portrait are shown in full either way. Watching it adds one separate card, labelled as a bonus. Like every ad in the app, it is served without personalisation: AdMob may still use a device identifier to cap repeats and prevent fraud, never to profile you. There is no in-app purchase and no subscription.

Duo: comparing two portraits happens entirely on your two devices. The portrait summary travels inside the link or the code you share yourself, in the part after the “#”, which browsers never send to a server. Nothing is uploaded, nothing about your duo is stored on our side and we never see it. No account is needed and we build no profile about you.
